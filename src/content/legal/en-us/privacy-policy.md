## 1. Who is responsible {#controller}

The data controller under the LGPD is Leone Santos Martins, a natural person. Contact: legal@empirecommunity.org. This policy applies to the empirecommunity.org website. The community's other services have their own policies.

## 2. What this website does not do {#what-the-site-does-not-do}

The website has no forms, accounts or registration, sets no cookies of its own, keeps no access logs, and does not sell user data. Item 7 says who data is shared with.

## 3. Technical records {#technical-records}

The website's server keeps no access logs. It keeps only minimal error records, for diagnosis. These records may contain the connection address of whoever was accessing the website when the error occurred, are replaced on a short rotation, and are lost when the website's server is recreated. The automated service that updates the member count stores only that community number and technical data about its own update, with no visitor data.

## 4. Usage statistics {#statistics}

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to learn how many visits the website's pages receive. The Support page and the community rules page (/discord) do not use this tool, and visits to them are not counted. This tool does not use cookies or local storage in your browser and does not identify people.

## 5. Language selection {#language}

When you open the main address, Cloudflare identifies the country of your connection to send you to the Portuguese or English version. The website does not keep this information.

## 6. Contact by email {#email-contact}

If you write to us, we will use what you send in order to reply.

## 7. Who data is shared with {#sharing}

Only the providers needed to run the website: the hosting provider and Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes the connection between you and the website and keeps its own records, outside this website's control. External services linked from the website have their own terms and privacy policies.

## 8. Legal basis {#legal-basis}

We process error records and statistics on the basis of legitimate interest, to keep the website running and secure, and messages to respond to your contact request.

## 9. How long {#retention}

The website's server keeps no access logs, and error records are replaced on a short rotation. Other data is kept while in use and is deleted on request, except what the law requires us to keep.

## 10. Your rights {#rights}

Under the LGPD, you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, portability or deletion of your data. Just write to legal@empirecommunity.org.

## 11. People listed on the website {#people-listed}

Only adults who have given recorded consent appear on the Organization page, and only with the data each person has authorized. To withdraw consent or request removal, write to legal@empirecommunity.org; we remove that data from the website and from the files we publish. This website's code and data are in a public repository on GitHub, which keeps the history of every change. Because of this, even after removal, what was already published remains in that history and in copies of the repository made by other people. Each person is told this before being listed.

## 12. Changes {#changes}

This policy may change, and the effective date will be updated.
