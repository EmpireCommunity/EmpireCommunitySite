## 3. Donations {#donations}

Donating is voluntary. Donating gives you no right to anything and is neither a purchase nor an exchange: a donation does not buy a product, a service or a benefit.

Payment is made on Mercado Pago, under its rules. The minimum amount is R$ 5 and we set no maximum; above R$ 5,000, we ask you to type the amount again to confirm it. All donations go to one general community fund: a donation cannot be earmarked for a specific project.

Refunds and chargebacks follow Mercado Pago's rules.

The figures on the Support page are built like this: donations come from Mercado Pago; Mercado Pago's fees enter the balance at the monthly check; expenses and adjustments are recorded by the owner, with the name of the company paid (or just "Person" when it is a private individual). The balance is calculated from our own ledger and checked once a month by the owner; it is not read from the Mercado Pago account. A correction is marked as corrected, and the number of withdrawn records is published. Once a year, the totals of the Mercado Pago statement back the published figures.

Anyone who wishes can ask, through a Discord ticket, for the supporter role on Discord and for access to EmpireMuse's limited features, such as radio connections and 24h playback. The team grants them by hand, at its discretion, regardless of the amount donated, and may end them at any time. Such a request is not linked to a payment or to a public name.

Do not use the public name to pass yourself off as the community, its staff or anyone else. The owner can take any public name off the list without notice.
